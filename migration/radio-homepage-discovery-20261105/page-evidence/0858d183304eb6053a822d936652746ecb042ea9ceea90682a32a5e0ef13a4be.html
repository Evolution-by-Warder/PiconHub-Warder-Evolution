About Radio 2moro

Radio 2Moro – Sawt El Ghad 1620 AM is the No.1 Arabic Radio station in Australia with 80% listenership (WMI, 2015).

Radio 2Moro is a twin Radio station to Sawt El Ghad-Lebanon (No.1 FM Radio station in the Middle East),and a sister company to World Media International, the distributor of the most popular Arabic ,Greek & Italian Pay-TV channels in Australia.

Radio 2Moro is ‘ TOP’ of the dial broadcasting since December 2002 24hours a day 7 days a week Sydney Wide, Australia Wide and World Wide.
