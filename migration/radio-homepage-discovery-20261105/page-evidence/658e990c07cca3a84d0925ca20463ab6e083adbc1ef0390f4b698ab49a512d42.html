About

While the radio station itself has expanded, but the aim hasn't changed. Radio Foorti still plays a wide variety of music, ranging from golden classics to the latest song released from the top artists in Bangladesh, even mixing the hottest international tracks into the playlist. Through its network of friendly radio jockeys, constant audience interaction via text messages and special features allowing fans to get closer to their favorite stars, Radio Foorti has helped redefine the FM culture and has helped usher in a new way for music to be heard and for entertainment to be enjoyed. Radio Foorti is now live, 24 hours round the clock for listeners to be able to enjoy new and upcoming music anytime they want. For the ones who want to be a part of this pleasant progress, Radio Foorti has its door wide open.
