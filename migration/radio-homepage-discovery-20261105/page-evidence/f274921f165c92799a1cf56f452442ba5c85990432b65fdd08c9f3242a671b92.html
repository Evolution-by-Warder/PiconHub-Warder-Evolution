Cape Breton's First Christian FM Radio Station

Proudly transmitting the Good News of Jesus Christ across the airwaves of Cape Breton island.

Listen Now

CICB Praise FM is a Christ-centred FM radio station that is dedicated to sharing the life-changing message of Jesus Christ through music, testimony, and teaching - in Cape Breton and the wider world.

Tune in using the frequency 90.7

Music that will move your soul!

Listen to artists like Big Daddy Weave, Lauren Daigle, for KING & COUNTRY, Mercy Me, Hillsongs United, and many others to hear inspirational music that will move your soul and your feet!

Featured artist: Matt Maher

A message that will change your life...

We believe in the transforming love of Jesus Christ...

"For this is how much God loved the world — He gave His one and only, unique Son as a gift. So now everyone who believes in Him will never perish but experience everlasting life." (John 3:16 TPT)

Testimonies of what God is doing in your community...

God is moving in Cape Breton and we know that "faith comes through hearing, that is, hearing the Good News about Christ ..."
‍
That's why it's so important we hear about what Jesus is doing in our towns, cities, and across the entire island of Cape Breton!
