Vikentis Radio

Keep the signal on

Vikentis Radio plays independent artists around the clock, and it does it without label money and without interrupting the music with ads. Everything you hear is paid for by the people who listen to it.

Running it costs real money every month: streaming bandwidth, the servers and software behind the player, the schedule and the submission system, and the hours spent listening to every track that arrives.

If the station has played something you loved, a donation is what keeps it on air — and keeps submissions free for every artist who sends us a track.

Supporters are also invited into our platform, where you can chat, connect and network directly with the artists we feature.

Keep the signal on

There is no label behind Vikentis Radio and no advertising inside it. Everyone who hosts a show, answers the messages and keeps the server running does it for free.

What is not free is the streaming, the bandwidth, the software, and the hours spent listening to every track that arrives. That is what a donation pays for — and it is what keeps submissions free for every independent artist who sends us music.

Supporters are also invited into our platform, where you can chat, connect and network directly with the artists we feature.
