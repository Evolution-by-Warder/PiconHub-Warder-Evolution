Welcome to DanceGroove Radio

The Internet's #1 80's Funk, Soul & Boogie Station

The DanceGroove Radio team is a collective of DJs who have one passion in common: the love of funk, soul and disco from the 70s and 80s. To share this passion with other people, this non-profit radio station started some years ago.

The station offers an automated continuous 24/7 broadcast along with live presentations from DJs at night. Each DJ brings their own personality and music tastes to their shows, making our broadcasts fun to listen to.
