. . . Not the​ same old stuff . . .

Whether you call it Easy Listening, Beautiful Music, Good Music, Light Music, Mood Music, Light Classics and, yes, even Elevator Music . . . This is the place for you...

Enjoy the station? Your purchase of Hopelessly Romantic helps us keep the music playing.

Welcome to Easy Music + Plus. We bring you back to a world where melody reigns and the songs never go out of style. Easy Music + Plus is your dedicated streaming home for the lush orchestras, soothing instrumentals, and timeless arrangements that once graced the airwaves and FM dials across America. 

This online station is a tribute to the golden era of easy listening radio. Whether you're seeking a gentle backdrop for your day or a nostalgic journey through velvet harmonies, Easy Music + Plus invites you to rediscover the charm of a genre built on grace, melody, and pure listening pleasure.

Easy Music + Plus features a vibrant mix of the best Easy Listening favorites ever recorded.

For melodies you'll remember humming along to, and the arrangements you'll never forget, make Easy Music + Plus your constant musical companion.

The most memorable melodies . . . the  best orchestrations . . . the best sound quality!
