Welcome to Life Impact Network Ministries' Eternity Gospel Radio FM 96.5 MHz

The glory of the LORD rising from Limbe, the Gates of the Nation. The Glory of the LORD shines from this Radio station displacing the thick darkness that covers the earth, and gross darkness that enslaves God’s people.

Who we are

We are an interdenominational ministry that exists to bring people and communities into relationship with Jesus Christ through the media;connect, educate and impact people and communities through Godly principles and resources.

About Us

What we do

Our mission is to equip and help the nations to mature in Jesus Christ and serve humanity(Ephesians 4:12 & 13), through becoming better leaders in the church, businesses, family, professional careers and the government.

Find out more

How you help

Your finances for given projects;
Radio and TV equipment
Recorders, video cameras and projectors
Computers,laptops,printers, photocopiers and accessories
Outdoor evangelism equipment and evangelism caravan
Bibles, tracts and other Christian books
Professional/Technical training

How you help

Make a difference

We working to ensure better outcomes for children in orphanages and shelters across the world.

How your donation is invested

Well-designed plans and supports

Counseling, spiritual upliftment, Bible studies, leadership training and mentoring;retreats,seminars and conferences;

Growth and development

Growing and reaching further areas and more people with the gospel of Jesus and with programs on the family, governance, agriculture, health, education, business, news, moral values, sports, youths and leadership empowerment;

Transition and growing independence

Creating business and employment opportunities for the youths through education, training opportunities in auditing techniques, recording and programming, journalism skills, dramatic and musical arts as a way of alleviating poverty in the nation;
