KARMA the Kat - delightful and quirky weekly cartoon 

Karma the Kat is a weekly cartoon featured on the Radiodowntown.ca website and online magazine. Created by Debi Stoness, the strip follows Karma—a sassy, street-smart cat whose sharp humor and clever storytelling lead her through hilarious, thought-provoking scenarios. With vibrant illustrations and a mischievous personality, Karma delivers a delightful, lighthearted escape that fans love returning to each week. Brightening up every reader's week with playful commentary on life and love, this fan-favorite feature combines witty humor with poignant observations on the human condition. Whether you are a longtime follower or discovering the comic for the first time, Karma the Kat offers a refreshing dose of joy available anytime in print or online.

Our Feature Artist

Bée’s Musical Journey

Early Beginnings: Raised near Düsseldorf, Germany, Bée started out singing for school friends before gaining confidence as a lead vocalist by performing live with local bands.
Sounds of Silence (2020): She co-founded an acoustic trio with musicians Sven Neukranz and Heinz Schäfer. Their acoustic cover of Iyeoka's "Simply Falling" achieved widespread international radio airplay.
Solo Transition (2022): Renowned blues-rock artist Mick Simpson and producer Andy Littlewood (Madearsproduction UK) approached Bée to shift her focus from cover songs to original material for a solo album.
First Solo Releases (2023): She launched her solo career with two singles: "Shut the World Away" (May 30, 2023) and "Slow Moving Train" (June 15, 2023).
