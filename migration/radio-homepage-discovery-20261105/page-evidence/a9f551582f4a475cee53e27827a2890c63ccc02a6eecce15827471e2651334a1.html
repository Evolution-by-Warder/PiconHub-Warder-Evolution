About Radio Melodia

Radio Melodia Adelaide is your premier destination for Greek music and news, bringing the vibrant culture of Greece to the heart of Adelaide. We broadcast Greek music and news on 152.275 ΜΗz, FM VHF in the area of Adelaide and the sourounding suburbs. You can listen us in the area of Adelaide with a radio scanner or global from internet. To get the special radio that tunes the Greek stations, call us at 70710821.

Radio Melodia is broadcasting from 2014 and its purpose is the promotion of the Greek culture in the region of Adelaide. Advertise in Radio Melodia at the best rates on the market. The announcements for charity purposes in Melodia are free of charge.
