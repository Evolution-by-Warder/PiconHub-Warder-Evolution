Welcome to Canada At Large

Your Canadian Internet Radio for Canadian tunes.

Streaming from the capital of Canada, we're a private, non-commercial Canadian internet radio station celebrating the artistic sounds from coast to coast to coast.

Here, you'll discover a carefully curated collection of tranquil and atmospheric sounds. Canada At Large is a perfect companion for working, studying, or simply unwinding.

Our mission is to showcase the extraordinary Canadian musical talent flourishing throughout the nation, highlighting the vast creative landscape that makes Canada remarkable. This Canadian internet radio station experience is intentionally different: no political discourse, no troubling news, just the serene, cinematic and sometimes wild music that reflects our country's beauty and depth.
