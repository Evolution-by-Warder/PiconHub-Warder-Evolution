The Voice of Islam Radio Station

from Sydney, Australia - 87.6 FM

The Voice of Islam Radio Station broadcasts from Sydney, Australia on the following frequencies.

  • 87.6 FM
  • 173.350 MHz (Narrow Band)
  • 173.425 MHz (Narrow Band)
  • 107.9 FM (Only during the holy month of Ramadan)
Daily broadcast includes:
  • Recitation of the Holy Quran
  • Islamic lectures
  • Live broadcasts of Friday sermons
  • Local and international news
  • Radio documentaries
  • Talk shows and programs on contemporary topics
  • Trivia and competitions
