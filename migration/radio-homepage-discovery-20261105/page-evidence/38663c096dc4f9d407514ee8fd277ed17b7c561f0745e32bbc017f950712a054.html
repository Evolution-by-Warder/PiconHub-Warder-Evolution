Macarthur Breakfast with Ally Redondo

Born and bred in Macarthur, Ally Redondo started talking when she was 8 months old.. and quite frankly never stopped! Hilarious, witty and relatable... what you hear is what you get with Ally!

A woman of the people, official town crier of Campbelltown, Ambassador for the Wollondilly & the host of Macarthur Breakfast. When not on-air, you'll find Ally hosting all of the best local events or out supporting businesses and charities in Macarthur.
