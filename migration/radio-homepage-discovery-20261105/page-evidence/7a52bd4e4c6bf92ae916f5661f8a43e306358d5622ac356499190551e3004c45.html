What the hell is NoLife-radio?

An independent web-radio dedicated to all the lovers of videogames music from the early days till now.

The radio playlist is simply shuffled between single tracks selected by DJ Cookie, foukevin, Dowseman, Syl and perin03.

Special thanks to serial for code, tools and admin. Website design by perin03 and Syl.

All the programs are advertising-free.
And if you really like what you hear donate using the donation widget! We'd like to add a second server with your donation. More servers = more listeners!

And of course, if you already have a server and want to relay the shoutcast, tell us and we'll add your server to our playlist.

Legal information

All the songs that you can listen on the NoLife-radio are protected by their author's rights. It is forbidden to use these songs except for a private and personal listening. The use of music material out of the family context is strictly prohibited.
