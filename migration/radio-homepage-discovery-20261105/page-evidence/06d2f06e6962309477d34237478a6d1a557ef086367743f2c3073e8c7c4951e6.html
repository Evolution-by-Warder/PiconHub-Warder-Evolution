THE KSUN66 WEEKENDS

When the weekend arrives, THE ROAD shifts into a different gear. Four exclusive shows bring fresh ideas, deeper playlists and carefully crafted themes that go beyond our daily programming. From the unpredictable selections of THE TEST and the West Coast atmosphere of K-SUN BOULEVARD to the nostalgic fun of THE DINER PARTY and the signature journey of ALL FOR ONE, every show has its own identity and loyal audience. No reruns of the daily schedule. Just original programmes, carefully curated for listeners who believe weekends deserve something special.
