From the Black See to the World - Feel the Power!

             Radio Yujen Briag — Power Station is an independent online electronic music and progressive house radio station streaming high-quality electronic music 24/7 worldwide.

                                                                      The station has roots tracing back to Bulgaria’s southern Black Sea coast in the 1990s.

           This is a modern re-imagined revival of a local radio station known for its 90s dance music, carrying the memory of summer nights, dance music, and the free spirit of Burgas.

    The mission is to deliver carefully curated, high-quality electronic music from a new generation of artists, while preserving the spirit of the original “sea breeze from Burgas” radio culture.

                                         The station stands at the intersection of memory and modernity — a voice for a generation that believes creativity is meant to be shared.
