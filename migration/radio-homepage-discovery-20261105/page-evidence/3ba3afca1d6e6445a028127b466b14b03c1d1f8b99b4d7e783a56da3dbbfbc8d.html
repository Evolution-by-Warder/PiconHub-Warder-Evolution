About

Welcome to The Bull FM88 Charters Towers’ ONLY country music Station!

The Bull FM88 – Kickin’ Country…playing the latest and greatest country music all day, every day!

Tune into FM 88.0 to catch your favorite country music and news!

The Bull FM88 Mission

Here at The Bull FM88, we operate on a 1 Watt Low Powered Open Narrowcasting
(LPON) radio licence, playing nothing but country music to the Charters Towers community.

We are a small independent radio station servicing the local community and businesses – supporting those who support us!

The Bull FM88 is a subsidiary of Margflow Media.
