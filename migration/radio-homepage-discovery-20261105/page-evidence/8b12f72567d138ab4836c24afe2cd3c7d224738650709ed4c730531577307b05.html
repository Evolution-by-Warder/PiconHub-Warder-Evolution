Who We Are

The story behind The Lounge Hour

Our Story

The Lounge Hour was born from a simple idea: the world needs more quiet. In a world of noise, notifications, and endless stimulation, we wanted to create a space where time slows down.

We are an independent internet radio station dedicated to jazz piano, lounge music, and the kind of sound that makes you exhale. Broadcasting 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, from France.

Our Music

Every track on The Lounge Hour is carefully selected for its atmosphere. We play jazz piano, lofi jazz, smooth instrumental, and ambient lounge — all curated to create a seamless sonic experience perfect for sleep, focus, and relaxation.

Our entire library is sourced from royalty-free music, ensuring a clean and uninterrupted listening experience.

Our Mission

To be your most trusted companion during the quiet hours. Whether you're working late, falling asleep, or simply need a moment of calm — The Lounge Hour is always there.

Cookie Policy

How we use cookies — last updated 2025

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

Cookies We Use

The Lounge Hour uses only strictly necessary cookies. No advertising, tracking or third-party marketing cookies.

  • Session cookies — to maintain your volume preference during your visit
  • Netlify analytics — anonymous, aggregated traffic data only
  • Google Fonts — font loading only, no persistent cookies

What We Do NOT Use

  • Google Analytics or similar tracking tools
  • Facebook Pixel or social media tracking
  • Advertising networks or retargeting cookies

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. This will not affect your ability to listen to The Lounge Hour.
