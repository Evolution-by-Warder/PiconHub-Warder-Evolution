Magic Radio - 80s Internet Radio Streaming Online

Magic Radio is an independent online radio station, on air since 2002, dedicated to the greatest hits of the 80s, with occasional nods to the 70s and 90s.

Our mission is to bring that golden era back to life - streaming 24/7, free of charge, and with no intrusive ads.

Quality

Three stations, one player

Magic Radio plays the greatest hits of the 80s around the clock, with occasional nods to the 70s and 90s. Proxima is a second stream with its own selection, and Bonus serves golden hits with the Orban sound. You can switch between them at any time, right from the player above.

Sound quality up to lossless FLAC

Every station streams in AAC 96 kbps, MP3 192 and MP3 320, so you can match the quality to your connection. Magic Radio also broadcasts in FLAC - lossless audio, identical to the original recording, which very few internet radio stations offer. Your choice is remembered from one visit to the next.

Listen everywhere

Play it straight from this page in any browser, or install the free app for iPhone, iPad and Android, which also runs on CarPlay and Android Auto in the car. Magic Radio is available on Alexa too, on the French skill store. No account, no registration, no intrusive advertising and no tracking.

Frequently asked questions

Is Magic Radio the same as Magic Radio UK?

No. Magic Radio (magic-radio.net) is an independent internet radio station founded in 2002. It has no connection with Magic Radio UK, the British station operated by Bauer Media.

Is Magic Radio free?

Yes. Magic Radio is free to listen to, on the website and in the apps, with no account, no registration and no intrusive advertising. It is a non-profit personal project, supported by donations on Tipeee.

What music does Magic Radio play?

Mostly the greatest hits of the 80s, 24 hours a day, with occasional tracks from the 70s and 90s. Two other stations share the player: Proxima, with its own selection, and Bonus, golden hits with the Orban sound.

Does Magic Radio stream in lossless quality?

Yes. The Magic Radio station broadcasts in FLAC, lossless audio. Every station is also available in AAC 96 kbps, MP3 192 kbps and MP3 320 kbps.

Can I listen to Magic Radio in my car?

Yes. The free Magic Radio app for iPhone and Android works with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.

Is Magic Radio available on Alexa?

Yes, on the French Alexa skill store: enable the Magic Radio skill, then say "Alexa, ouvre Magic Radio".

Can I listen with my own player, such as VLC?

Yes. The player links to an .m3u playlist for each station and quality, which opens in VLC, foobar2000 or most other audio players.

Is Magic Radio licensed?

Yes. Magic Radio broadcasts under licences from SACEM (copyright) and SPRE (neighbouring rights of performers and phonogram producers).
