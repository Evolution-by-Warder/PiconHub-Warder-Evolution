Yacht Rock is a style of smooth and sophisticated pop music originating in Los Angeles from the late-70s to the mid-80s. It's often thrown into the "soft rock" bucket, but yacht rock draws more from rnb and jazz influences than it does from folk, country or ballads.

It started in late 2016 with a penchant for collecting dollar-bin yacht rock records.  A bunch of Mixcloud sets and home demos later I found myself at local radio station, North West FM, and in 2019 the North West Coast was born.


After the pandemic blew that to pieces I built Radio PVS and Smooth Sailing Radio in my front room and brought the NWC along for the ride. Over 260 episodes deep.
