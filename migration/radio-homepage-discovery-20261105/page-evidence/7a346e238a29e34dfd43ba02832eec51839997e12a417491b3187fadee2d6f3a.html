About the station
Yumi Co. Radio

A 24/7 independent web radio focused on Future Funk and Japanese City Pop, with Anime Groove, Nu Disco, Vaporwave, Synthwave and related styles in the rotation.

What you will hear
Future Funk & Friends

The main rotation, including Anime Groove, Nu Disco, Synthwave and related sub-genres.

Japanese City Pop

Music from the 1970s, 1980s and 1990s, presented in a short hourly segment with a retro Japanese advertisement.

Vaporwave

A short Vaporwave segment appears around the middle of each hour.

                    About Live Chat
                    

                        

                            
                        

                        

                            

                                Chat with other Yumi Co. Radio listeners in #General, #Music and #Shitposting. Choose a nickname and connect. Double-click someone in the user list to open a private conversation. Reloading the page clears the messages shown in your browser.
                            

                        

                    

                

                

                    Features & Tips
                    

                        

                            
Channels and Activity

                            
Switch public channels with the tabs. In Chat Options, you can move join and leave notices to a separate, read-only Activity tab. Turn the option off to see them in their original channels again.

                        

                        

                            
Accounts and Nicknames

                            
You can chat without an account. A Yumi Co. Radio account reserves your nickname for use on the website and Android app. An account nickname cannot be changed. If you already have a reserved chat nickname, you can claim it with its current chat password.

                        

                        

                            
File Uploads

                            
Sign in to your account to share images, video, audio or PDF files in a channel or private message. Use the Upload button, drag and drop, or paste from your clipboard. The limit is 200 MB per file and 500 MB per day; use /quota to check your allowance. Files are removed automatically after about 24 hours. Anyone who has a file's link can open it, including a link shared in a private message.

                        

                        

                            
Links and Previews

                            
Images, GIFs and supported audio or video links can appear directly in the chat. Links from YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify, Bandcamp, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch and Streamable may show a player. Imgur and Gyazo links may show an image preview.

                        

                        

                            
Status and Options

                            
Set your status to Online, Away or Busy from the toolbar. After 10 minutes without activity on the site, it changes to Away automatically. Chat Options also lets you adjust the appearance, image preview size and notification sounds.

                        

                        

                            
Useful Actions

                            
Right-click a message or link to copy it, paste into the message box, or open a link in a new tab.
