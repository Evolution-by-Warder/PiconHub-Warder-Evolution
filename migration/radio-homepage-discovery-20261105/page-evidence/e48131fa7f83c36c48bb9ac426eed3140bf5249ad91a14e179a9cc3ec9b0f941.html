Bringing the East Kimberley
•••Together•••

Waringarri Media Aboriginal Corporation operates the East Kimberley’s only locally based radio station and media organisation. We are WA’s oldest Aboriginal community radio station, that is proudly Indigenous owned and serving the whole region.

We broadcast out of our studios in Kununurra for a 300km radius to major towns, remote grazing stations, and outback communities across the WA and NT border region on 693AM and soon 102.1FM.

Waringarri Media plays a vital role in keeping the local community informed, with a high visibility covering regional news and events, online audio and video streaming, and a strong social media presence.

Learn More

Our Shows

Waringarri Media is the region’s leading source of local news and information, music and entertainment. Our morning talk shows keep our local community informed and stimulated with interviews, local views and perspectives. This is sprinkled with a great mix of music, playing classic and new rock hits and popular music. We also play the best of country and Indigenous artists from across the Kimberley and Australia.

See Programs and Schedule for more information about all of our shows.
