# PiconHub 0.1.1 branding

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. Transparent PNGs retain their alpha.

Production paths relative to this repository:

- `plugin/src/Plugins/Extensions/ChocholousekPicons/images/warder-emblem.png` — 640 × 640; displayed at the full height of the initial preview.
- `plugin/src/Plugins/Extensions/ChocholousekPicons/images/plugin.png` — 100 × 40; plugin browser icon.
- `plugin/src/Plugins/Extensions/ChocholousekPicons/images/plugin_fhd.png` — 150 × 60; Full HD plugin browser icon.

The generated originals were resized with Lanczos for runtime assets, without changing the artwork. The preview contains the square emblem proportionally; after choosing a background it shows the actual picon preview.

## Emblem prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Production asset for the existing PiconHub by Warder Enigma2 television plugin. Create ONE spectacular premium emblem on a truly transparent background, near-square composition with a tall stacked silhouette that fills 90% of the canvas. At the top center a front-facing polished chrome cybernetic skull with glowing red eyes, encircled by an electric cyan orbital halo, dark navy highlights, refined luminous blue circuitry and restrained spark accents. Below the skull in large impeccably legible metallic white and cyan lettering exact text "PICONHUB". Beneath in smaller elegant silver lettering exact text "by Warder". Match high-end futuristic dark blue television interface, strong crisp silhouette and controlled luminous edges. Skull occupies upper two thirds; wordmark lower third. No mockup, no device, no rectangular background, no additional slogan, no other text, no tiny details beyond silhouette. Transparent exterior around the entire logo. Render finished polished 3D metallic brand emblem, not a screenshot.

## Icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Reference image is the new PiconHub brand emblem. Create a matching compact production PLUGIN ICON intended to be displayed at only 100x60 pixels on a dark blue TV plugin list. Landscape 5:3 composition. A simplified bold chrome cybernetic skull with red eyes and bright cyan halo on the LEFT, large simple highly legible "PICON" over "HUB" stacked on the RIGHT in bold metallic white and cyan capitals. Use same identity and palette as reference. No "by Warder" text here, no extra small type, no thin decorative circuitry, no slogan. Maximize useful occupied space with very tight transparent outer margins. Crisp strong large shapes for small size. A dark navy rounded badge behind these elements is allowed, but the exterior background must be truly transparent. Finished premium icon, no mockup, no screenshot.

## Satellite artwork

The 53 satellite/provider icons are original assets from FullHDGlass Warder Evolution, not generated logos. `images/selectors/sources.json` records the original filenames and SHA256 values. Selection identifiers remain distinct even where two menu items share an image.
