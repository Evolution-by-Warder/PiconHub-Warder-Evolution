Warder background and size publication policy
Owner instruction, 4 November 2026

Every satellite position and every provider must offer Transparent, White and Black at every advertised resolution. Physical satellite positions must offer 50x30, 220x132 and 400x240, each with all three backgrounds. Retained legacy/provider/terrestrial compatibility packages must have all three backgrounds at every resolution they advertise; never silently hide a background in either client.

Use the original frozen Warder BLACK and WHITE MASTER templates and approved original logo layers. Keep transparent artwork derived from the approved source. Preserve approved proportions, centring, identities and unrelated existing PNG bytes. Do not invent service references or redraw approved artwork.

Before activation, fill missing variants from verified approved source material, verify their dimensions, archive inventory, public byte sizes and SHA256, and exercise both client installers. If verified source material is unavailable, report the missing item and block incomplete publication rather than inventing artwork or hiding an option.

Both clients enforce satellite_variant_policy in the shared manifest and validate complete three-background coverage, including provider groups. The live catalogue has 492 packages: 49 physical position selectors with 441 satellite variants, and 164 advertised selector/resolution groups with all three backgrounds.

34.5W (345W, receiver orbital namespace CB7) and 50.0E (500E, receiver orbital namespace 1F4) are supported in PiconHub 0.1.43 and FullHDGlass 1.0.66. Saved profiles and old FullHDGlass SATLIST indexes are preserved. Thirteen previously blocked service identity conflicts remain blocked.

Verification uses real client installation code in a host test environment. This release does not claim a new physical receiver test.
